Every new event starts with your team’s name, logo and colours. Set them once in Settings; each event can still change its own.

Choose your DisplayLink Plus template folder

Board slides and countdown backgrounds come from your DisplayLink Plus templates (.tpl). On the DisplayLink Plus PC that's the folder DisplayLink Plus keeps them in; on another computer, any folder holding copies.
